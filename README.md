**Neon Shores** — an open-world crime game in Solmar City, a golden-hour coastal city of palm-lined boulevards, neon art-deco hotels, a working port, and a causeway running north to a lighthouse island. You are Luz Marino, back in town for her turquoise coupe and one last string of jobs for Coco at Club Azul.

Play it on foot or behind the wheel: steal anything with four wheels, outrun a five-star wanted system with pursuing SMPD interceptors, and work a five-mission story chain — a pickup at the West Docks, a timed causeway dash through three checkpoints, and a finale that ends only when you shake the heat and reach the Lighthouse Garage. After the story, endless odd jobs pay cash. Everything is procedural: the city, the canvas-painted facades and signs, and even the WebAudio engine, siren and sea are generated in code. Three.js is vendored; there are no other assets and no network requests.

**Controls:** WASD move/drive · mouse drag camera · Shift run · F enter/leave car · Space/J shove or handbrake · P pause · Enter/click confirms screens.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Or open `game/index.html` from any static server — it runs fully offline.
